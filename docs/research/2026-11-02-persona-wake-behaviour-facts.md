### 1.3 Reply schema (persona.rs:345-433 `reply_schema`; type `PersonaReply` :244-262)
Required keys: `say[]` {room (enum of allowed rooms), text}; `start_self_run` (null or {brief <=1000, repo `Org/repo`, campaign (enum of campaigns in force; with none, forced null :390-392), for_item}); `remember[]` (<=8; {sort, text <=1000 B, about[] <=8, salience_permille}); `settle[]` (<=8; {item, outcome done|declined|wrong, why <=300}); `urgency_permille` (null or 0-1000). Optional `tempo_vote_beat_s`. Field descriptions: REMEMBER_DESCRIPTION persona.rs:341 ("What you must still know when this conversation is gone ... Leave empty when nothing here is worth keeping."), SETTLE_DESCRIPTION :342, FOR_ITEM_DESCRIPTION :343. Decode rule (persona.rs:1275-1286 `decode_reply`, :1248-1272 `memory_in_bounds`): every `remember.about` must be one of the ids in that turn's "People you may name" list, every `settle.item` an open id shown that turn; otherwise `Undecodable` (refused and told back; third refusal for a batch ends the session, REFUSALS_PER_BATCH = 3 :84).

### 1.4 What a turn does not contain
- No room tail on a scheduled wake: `earlier` lines come only from `delivery.context` of pending deliveries (persona.rs:1778-1780); with none, `render_input` prints none. Ruling wake-reads-room-tail (admitted 2026-11-01 20:12Z) calls this the next cut; origin/main has no Matrix history read in `render` (other branches not searched).
- No stewarded-repo activity, no PRs or campaign staleness: `render_responsibilities` shows steward notes (static prose) plus open follow-ups. Cuts 1 and 3 of stewardship-as-body and the follow_up pressures-from-stewardship-state are not present in the code or in the live files.
- Persona-state `agencyPressure` and `thoughtMemory` reach the Mind only inside the system-prompt JSON dump (section 4), unchanged since 2026-10-31T09:00:01Z.
- Heartbeat gating (heartbeat.rs:1-24): exactly one Persona takes a turn per beat (`tempo::pick` over idle released Minds by waiting time, bent by self-declared urgency and by a member addressing her). Off-beat launches only for the operator tagging or naming her (once per message) or her own Self run having ended since her last turn. `wakes()` (heartbeat.rs:440-444): the operator or another roster Mind addressing her by tag or whole-word name wakes her; a non-Mind member does not.
